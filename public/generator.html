Reinstates that device's revoked license and re-enables its sync access. If the device is showing the activation screen afterwards, it enters its license key again. Safe to run twice - use that to retry if the platform step timed out.

📅 Change a license expiry date

📲 Move a license to a new device
Keeps the end date. Everything the old device held moves with it, and the old device stops being licensed the next time it checks in. Afterwards the customer opens NexaPOS on the new device and enters their key (or taps Restore). This does not bring back their shop data - that comes from a backup.
